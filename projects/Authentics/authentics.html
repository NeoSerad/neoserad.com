Authentics - 2 for $80

"Your everyday basics made better."

A speculative advertisement for the Australian streetwear brand Factorie and their Authentics collection.

When I approach the creation of spec work, I always pursue brands that I have a personal connection with. I personally have purchased from the Authentics collection, which led me to wanting to create a fashion ad specific for their line.

I thought it would be an interesting challenge and structure to include each of Factorie's collections, but then conceptually (and literally) zoom into Authentics as the primary focus of the ad.
