Authentics - 2 for $80

"Your everyday basics made better."

A speculative advertisement for the Australian streetwear brand Factorie and their Authentics collection.

When I approach the creation of spec ads, I always pursue brands that I have a personal connection with. I personally have purchased from the Authentics collection, which led me to wanting to create a fashion ad specific for their line.

I thought it would be an interesting challenge and structure to include each of Factorie's collections, but then conceptually (and literally) zoom into Authentics as the primary focus of the ad. This meant vectorising each logo manually to ensure a high quality composite and render.

Showcasing a variety of the Authentics collection meant I needed to select over 50 different high quality product images from the website. This ensured a diverse and visually appealing product line, that stayed on point with the tempo of the house track.

The main typography effect blends the fill of their expressive blue in the background with an outline overlayed on top of the masked models. This results in the effective yet creative presentation of both the text and the product simultaneously.

Additionally, this spec ad is partially responsible for landing my first motion design role at Vanilla Piercing & Jewellery.
